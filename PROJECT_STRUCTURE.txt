DATABASE STRUCTURE
==================

MySQL Database: gmpc_cms

Tables (8):
├── admins (User accounts)
│   ├── id, username, email, password
│   ├── full_name, role, status
│   └── last_login, created_at, updated_at
│
├── communities (Church communities)
│   ├── id, name, slug, description
│   ├── full_content, meeting_day, meeting_time
│   ├── meeting_location, image_url
│   └── status, display_order, timestamps
│
├── settings (Site settings)
│   ├── id, setting_key, setting_value
│   ├── setting_type, description
│   └── updated_at
│
├── events (Church events)
│   ├── id, title, description
│   ├── event_date, event_time, location
│   └── image_url, status, timestamps
│
├── sermons (Sermon recordings)
│   ├── id, title, preacher, sermon_date
│   ├── description, video_url, audio_url
│   └── thumbnail_url, series, timestamps
│
├── pages (Custom pages)
│   ├── id, page_name, page_slug
│   ├── title, meta_description, content
│   └── status, created_by, timestamps
│
├── sections (Homepage sections)
│   ├── id, section_name, section_key
│   ├── heading, subheading, content
│   └── button_text, button_link, image_url
│
└── media (Uploaded files)
    ├── id, filename, original_name
    ├── file_path, file_type, file_size
    └── uploaded_by, created_at
