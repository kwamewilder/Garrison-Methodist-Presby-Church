Israel

A special community within our church family

About Israel

Israel is a unique community within Garrison Methodist Presbyterian Church, dedicated to spiritual growth, fellowship, and service. Our members come together to support one another in faith and to make a positive impact in our church and wider community.

What We Do

  • Regular fellowship meetings and Bible study
  • Community outreach and service projects
  • Prayer and spiritual support for members
  • Participation in church events and programs
  • Mentorship and discipleship activities
  • Special cultural and spiritual celebrations

Our Mission

We strive to build strong relationships within our community while serving God and others. Through regular gatherings, prayer, and service, we seek to deepen our faith and make a meaningful contribution to the life of our church.

Get Involved

We welcome new members who share our passion for faith, fellowship, and service. Join us as we grow together in Christ and serve our community.
