Children Service

Nurturing young hearts for Jesus

About Children Service

Our Children Service at GMPC is a fun, safe, and engaging environment where children learn about God's love through age-appropriate activities, Bible stories, worship, and play. We believe in laying a strong spiritual foundation during these formative years.

What We Do

  • Interactive Bible lessons and storytelling
  • Age-appropriate worship and praise
  • Creative activities, crafts, and games
  • Scripture memory and character building
  • Special events and seasonal programs
  • Safe and caring environment with trained volunteers

Age Groups

We have classes for different age groups to ensure each child receives age-appropriate teaching:

  • Nursery: Ages 0-3
  • Preschool: Ages 4-5
  • Primary: Ages 6-9

Our Approach

We use creative teaching methods including stories, songs, drama, and hands-on activities to help children understand biblical truths and apply them to their daily lives. Our goal is to help children develop a personal relationship with Jesus Christ.
