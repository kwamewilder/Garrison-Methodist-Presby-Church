Men's Fellowship

Iron sharpening iron in faith and brotherhood

About Men's Fellowship

The Men's Fellowship at GMPC provides a supportive environment where men can grow in their faith, build strong friendships, and become godly leaders in their families, church, and community. We believe in challenging and encouraging each other to live out our calling as Christian men.

What We Do

  • Weekly Bible studies focused on men's issues
  • Prayer and accountability groups
  • Community service projects
  • Leadership development workshops
  • Men's breakfast meetings and social events
  • Mentorship and discipleship programs

Our Mission

We are committed to developing men of character, integrity, and faith who can make a positive difference in their spheres of influence. Through biblical teaching, authentic relationships, and practical application, we help men navigate the challenges of modern life while staying grounded in Christian principles.

Join Us

Whether you're seeking spiritual growth, looking for authentic male friendships, or wanting to become a better husband, father, or leader, Men's Fellowship offers a place to grow. All men are welcome to join our brotherhood.
