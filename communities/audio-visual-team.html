Audio Visual Team

Supporting worship through technology and media excellence

About Our Audio Visual Team

The Audio Visual Team at Garrison Methodist Presbyterian Church plays a vital role in enhancing our worship experience through the expert management of sound, video, and lighting systems. Our dedicated volunteers work behind the scenes to ensure that every service, event, and special gathering runs smoothly.

What We Do

  • Manage sound systems for all church services and events
  • Operate video projection and live streaming equipment
  • Handle lighting for worship services and special events
  • Record and archive sermons and special programs
  • Provide technical support for church presentations and multimedia
  • Maintain and upgrade audio-visual equipment

Who Can Join

We welcome anyone with an interest in audio-visual technology, whether you're experienced or just starting out. We provide training for new members and opportunities to grow your technical skills while serving the church.

Get Involved

If you're interested in joining the Audio Visual Team or learning more about what we do, please contact us through the church office. We meet regularly for training and service coordination.

Meeting Times

Training sessions held monthly

Service setup begins 30 minutes before each service

Skills Needed

  • Technical aptitude
  • Attention to detail
  • Reliability
  • Team player attitude
  • Willingness to learn

Contact

For more information, please reach out to the church office.
