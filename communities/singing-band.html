Singing Band

Making melody to the Lord with instruments and voices

About Singing Band

The Singing Band at GMPC is a vibrant music ministry that combines traditional hymns with contemporary praise through instrumental and vocal arrangements. Our band leads worship and provides special musical presentations that inspire and uplift the congregation.

What We Do

  • Lead worship during select services
  • Perform special musical numbers
  • Provide music for church events and programs
  • Participate in seasonal concerts and celebrations
  • Collaborate with other music ministries
  • Develop musical talents for God's glory

Instruments We Use

Our band includes a variety of instruments including keyboards, guitars, drums, bass, brass, and traditional instruments. We blend these together to create inspiring worship music.

Who Can Join

We welcome musicians and singers who want to use their talents to serve God. Whether you're experienced or still developing your skills, there's a place for you in our band. We provide coaching and opportunities to grow musically while serving.

Get Involved

If you play an instrument or love to sing, join us in making beautiful music for the Lord. Contact us to learn more about rehearsal schedules and how you can be part of our Singing Band.
