Youth Fellowship

Empowering the next generation for Christ

About Youth Fellowship

The Youth Fellowship at GMPC is a dynamic community of young people passionate about growing in faith and friendship. We provide opportunities for teens and young adults to connect with God, build meaningful relationships, and develop leadership skills.

What We Do

  • Weekly fellowship meetings with worship and teaching
  • Bible studies and discipleship groups
  • Community service and outreach projects
  • Social events and recreational activities
  • Annual retreats and conferences
  • Leadership development programs

Our Vision

We aim to raise up a generation of young people who are deeply rooted in Christ, equipped to make a difference in their schools, communities, and the world. Through relevant teaching, authentic worship, and genuine community, we help youth navigate life's challenges with biblical wisdom.

Get Involved

Whether you're looking for a place to belong, seeking answers to life's big questions, or wanting to serve others, Youth Fellowship is the place for you. Come experience a community where you can be yourself and grow in your faith.
