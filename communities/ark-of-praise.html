Ark of Praise

Lifting high the name of Jesus through worship

About Ark of Praise

Ark of Praise is a dynamic worship ministry at GMPC dedicated to creating an atmosphere where God's presence is experienced through powerful praise and worship. Our team of passionate worshippers leads the congregation into deeper intimacy with God through song.

What We Do

  • Lead congregational worship during services
  • Create an atmosphere for God's presence
  • Present special worship concerts and events
  • Minister through contemporary and traditional worship songs
  • Develop and mentor new worship leaders
  • Participate in prayer and intercession

Our Heart

We believe worship is more than singing songs - it's a lifestyle of honoring God with our whole hearts. Our aim is to usher the congregation into meaningful encounters with God, where hearts are transformed and lives are changed through worship.

Ministry Focus

Ark of Praise focuses on both vocal excellence and spiritual authenticity. We combine skillful musicianship with heartfelt worship to create an environment where people can freely express their love for God and experience His presence.

Join Us

If you have a heart for worship and want to use your voice to glorify God, we invite you to join Ark of Praise. We welcome vocalists and worship leaders who are committed to excellence and passionate about leading others into God's presence.
