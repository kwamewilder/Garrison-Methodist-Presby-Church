Women's Fellowship

United in faith, love, and service

About Women's Fellowship

The Women's Fellowship at GMPC is a vibrant community of women who gather to grow in faith, support one another, and serve our church and community. We believe in the power of godly women coming together to encourage, strengthen, and uplift each other.

What We Do

  • Weekly Bible studies and prayer meetings
  • Monthly fellowship gatherings
  • Community outreach and charity work
  • Mentorship programs for younger women
  • Annual retreats and conferences
  • Support for church events and programs

Our Mission

We are committed to nurturing spiritual growth, building strong Christian friendships, and making a positive impact in our community. Through prayer, study, and service, we seek to honor God and support one another in our various life roles as daughters, sisters, wives, mothers, and professionals.

Join Us

All women are welcome to join our fellowship, regardless of age or stage of life. Whether you're looking for spiritual growth, meaningful friendships, or opportunities to serve, you'll find a warm and welcoming community here.
