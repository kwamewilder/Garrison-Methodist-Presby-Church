About Our Choir

The GMPC Choir is a vibrant group of worshippers who use their voices to glorify God and lead the congregation in praise. Our choir performs traditional hymns, contemporary Christian music, and special anthems that enhance our worship services throughout the year.

What We Do

  • Lead congregational singing during worship services
  • Perform special musical presentations
  • Participate in seasonal programs and concerts
  • Provide music for weddings, funerals, and special events
  • Collaborate with other church music groups
  • Minister through song to the congregation and community

Who Can Join

Our choir welcomes all members of the congregation who love to sing and want to serve through music ministry. No audition is required - just a willing heart and a love for worship. We provide music training and voice development for those who need it.

Rehearsals

We rehearse weekly to prepare our music and grow together as a choir family. It's a wonderful time of fellowship, learning, and spiritual growth.
