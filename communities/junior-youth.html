Junior Youth

Building young disciples for Christ

About Junior Youth

The Junior Youth ministry at GMPC is designed for young people in their pre-teen and early teenage years. We provide a safe, fun, and engaging environment where young disciples can grow in their faith, build friendships, and discover their God-given purpose.

What We Do

  • Age-appropriate Bible studies and discussions
  • Fun activities and games that teach spiritual lessons
  • Service projects and community outreach
  • Leadership development and mentorship
  • Special events, retreats, and camps
  • Worship and praise sessions

Age Range

Our Junior Youth program welcomes young people typically between ages 10-14. We create a bridge between children's ministry and youth ministry, addressing the unique needs and questions of this important developmental stage.

Our Focus

We focus on helping junior youth understand their identity in Christ, develop strong moral foundations, build healthy friendships, and learn to apply biblical principles to everyday life situations.
